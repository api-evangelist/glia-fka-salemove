# Glia (fka SaleMove)

> Glia is a digital customer service / unified interaction management platform (formerly SaleMove) purpose-built for banks and credit unions. It combines voice AI, digital self-service, co-browsing, messaging, and video into a single ChannelLess engagement stack, plus Glia Functions — a serverless JavaScript platform — and mobile/web SDKs for embedding customer service into apps.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from apis.yml and the artifacts in this repository (no provider-published llms.txt was found on docs.glia.com).
